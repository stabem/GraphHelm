The edge case matters because the primary drive+pattern check alone cannot distinguish a
worktree that happens to be checked out at a path shaped like D:/graphhelm-target-<lane> from a
legitimate target dir - the explicit repositoryRoot comparison catches that structurally, by
comparing against the ACTUAL worktree this invocation is running from, not by trusting the drive
letter as a permanent fact about where worktrees live.
