# Task 8 report — legal holds and cryptographic erasure

Date: 2026-08-11
Task base: `f1ff6f6c9da1b60dd22266c9e291d9f7c3dd4120`

## Status

Implemented bounded, object-safe retention contracts and a crash-consistent PostgreSQL erasure saga. Requests bind exact scope, versioned policy, authenticated authority, safe reason code, ordered targets and idempotency. Prepared and finalized outcomes, provider revocation receipts, and legal-hold changes are authenticated. Provider revoke identities are domain-separated by full scope, operation and Evidence ID, so multi-target and cross-scope operations cannot collide.

Prepare locks and revalidates Evidence identity, ciphertext digest, classification, retention class, minimum age, prior availability and authenticated policy/authority before atomically recording the operation/events and making every target unreadable as `erasure_pending`. Provider failures remain pending. Reconciliation resumes from authenticated durable state. Finalize verifies the prepared receipt, every per-target provider receipt, provider epoch and aggregate final receipt before atomically publishing tombstones/events and marking Evidence `erased`. Physical ciphertext cleanup uses PostgreSQL transaction time, only after durable finalization and the cleanup delay, and returns the original receipt on exact retry.

Legal holds are authenticated append-only place/release records. Every read reconstructs and verifies hold history before deciding eligibility; a hold committed before prepare blocks erasure, while a late hold cannot resurrect pending or erased content. Tombstones preserve only approved identifiers, digests, classification, retention class, prior state, policy/authority and timestamps. Event/Evidence history remains append-only; guarded tables permit only the documented pending, finalize, receipt and cleanup transitions.
